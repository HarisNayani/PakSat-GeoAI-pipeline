# PakSat GeoAI Pipeline
## Zameen: Environmental Health & Economic Intelligence

### 60-Second Pitch

Air pollution is not just an environmental issue — it is a public health, operational, and economic risk. In Pakistan, decision-makers often have fragmented data: pollution readings in one place, hospital pressure elsewhere, and policy decisions without clear evidence of what is working. We built Zameen to solve that gap.

Zameen is a decision-support dashboard that combines PM2.5 telemetry, satellite and meteorological signals, and clinical context into one operational view. It helps cities monitor hotspot areas, assess patient risk, compare intervention scenarios, and estimate hospital resource needs. We also include policy simulation and economic translation so leaders can explore not just what is happening, but what actions could be most effective.

The system is designed with transparency in mind: it clearly labels demo data, separates observational policy scenarios from causal claims, and makes uncertainty visible. This makes it useful for real public-sector decision-making while staying honest about what the data can and cannot support.

Our goal is simple: turn air-quality evidence into better public-health preparedness, smarter policy decisions, and more resilient cities.

### 2-Minute Pitch

Our project, Zameen, addresses a critical gap in urban public health: governments and hospitals often lack a single, trusted view of how pollution is changing in real time and how that affects service demand.

We built a decision-support platform that brings together local air-quality observations, atmospheric and satellite data, clinical triage logic, and policy simulation. The platform helps officials answer practical questions: Where are PM2.5 levels rising? Which communities are most exposed? How might a policy intervention change outcomes? What resource planning is needed at the hospital level?

At the core of the system is a leakage-aware PM2.5 modeling pipeline. We use physics-informed features such as boundary-layer height, stagnation index, temperature, humidity, and lagged exposure patterns to build a time-aware predictive view. We also support a sensor-assisted model and an unmonitored satellite-based model, evaluated separately so the results are honest and transparent.

The dashboard has four key layers. First, environmental monitoring and evidence tracking. Second, clinical decision support for patient triage and risk escalation. Third, policy scenario analysis using aligned observational panels. Fourth, resource allocation to help hospitals plan beds, oxygen, and nebulizer capacity under pressure.

We also make the economics explicit. Instead of presenting a vague “benefit,” the platform translates intervention scenarios into localized cost assumptions and per-observation economic impact estimates, clearly labeling them as scenario-based rather than proven national savings.

Most importantly, we built the system around responsible AI principles. Demo data is clearly labeled, causal claims are treated as observational scenarios, and uncertainty is never hidden. This is essential for government-facing tools where trust and accountability matter.

Our vision is a public-health intelligence layer for cities: one that connects environmental evidence to operational response, supports better planning, and helps communities act earlier when air pollution becomes a health risk.

### Judge-Friendly Summary

Zameen is a GeoAI-powered environmental health intelligence platform for Pakistan. It combines air-quality evidence, satellite and meteorology inputs, patient risk triage, and policy/economic scenario modeling into a single decision-support dashboard. The project is designed for public agencies and hospitals that need actionable local insight without relying on opaque or unsupported claims.

We focus on three things: evidence, action, and transparency. The result is a practical decision-support tool that helps cities respond earlier and plan more effectively during pollution episodes.
